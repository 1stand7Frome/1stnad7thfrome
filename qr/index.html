Scouting Best Practices for QR Codes

🎯 Add Clear Call to Actions

Always print instructions next to the QR code, such as: "Scan to join 1st & 7th Frome Scouts!" or "Scan to donate."

📐 Sizing Guidelines

When printing on standard flyers, make the QR code at least 2 cm x 2 cm. For large banners, make sure the resolution is set higher (e.g., 800px) when downloading.

🧼 Keep High Contrast

Always maintain a high contrast between foreground and background. Standard white backgrounds work best for quick detection by modern smartphone camera apps.
