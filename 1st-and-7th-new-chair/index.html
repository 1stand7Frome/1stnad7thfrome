1st and 7th New Chair

GSL 14/12/2025 Group
Hi , I’m Paul and I am the new Chair person for 1st and 7th Frome ‘Jubilee’ Scout group.
 
Firstly I have to say a huge thank you to Emma who has been facilitating this role and done some outstanding work behind the scenes to support the group.
 
A little about me, I have some experience of working with a Scout Group previously having moved to Frome from Bristol. In my earlier days I have been a Cub Scout, Scout, Venture Scout and an Assistant Leader and this was such an important part of my life.
 
I have taken on this role in Frome to help support the amazing work that the leaders and Exec team are already doing to help develop the members in this incredible activity.
I will be posting quite a bit on behalf of the group and I would love to hear from anyone that would like to get involved to help see these youngsters develop.
 
It’s such a rewarding activity.
